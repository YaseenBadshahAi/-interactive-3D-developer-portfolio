About

Developer with a designer's eye

Example introduction — write your own story here.

I'm Alex Carter, a freelance frontend developer who lives at the intersection of engineering and design. I believe the best websites are built like products: every animation, layout choice, and millisecond of load time should serve the person using it.

My philosophy is simple — clarity over cleverness. I use motion and interactivity to guide attention, never to decorate. The result is websites that feel premium, load fast, and convert.
